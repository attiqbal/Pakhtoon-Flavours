About us

A family kitchen
from Khyber Pukhtunkhwa

We cook the dishes we grew up with in Khyber Pukhtunkhwa: Namkeen Gosht in the style of Peshawar's Namak Mandi, Painda from the south, and pulao from Bannu. Nothing is made in advance. Every order is cooked fresh for you.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Yes, with at least 24 hours' notice. You can cancel from your order link, or message us to change it. Late cancellations may not get a full refund, as ingredients and cooking will already have started.

I have an allergy. Can you help?

Please ask before you order: tell us in the chat or on WhatsApp and the cook will reply herself. You can also add a note at checkout.

Can you cook for a party or event?

Family portions feed several people, and for bigger orders just message us so we can plan it with you.
